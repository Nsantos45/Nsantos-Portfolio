Overview

Curriculum built like a lab — tested, iterated, deployed

I'm an Associate Director of Education and Instructional Designer with 7+ years of experience leading curriculum strategy, online and blended program development, and research-informed instructional innovation. I specialize in virtual lab development, AI-enhanced instruction, and gamified STEM and language learning. Most of the work below lives inside a virtual biology classroom I built to house it — an arcade of learning games, a science lab of simulations, and a wall of student work, all organized the way a real classroom would be.

Every project in the catalog below was designed against a real learning objective first — the game or simulation mechanic came second, chosen because it fit how the concept actually behaves (natural selection as a clicking simulation, codon translation as a typing challenge, osmosis as a defense game).
